Our Journey

A History of Restaurant

From humble beginnings to the city's most beloved restaurant every chapter written with passion.

2016
Humble Beginnings

Kwa Mathe opens its doors right in the heart of Juja, serving hearty, affordable meals tailored for hungry JKUAT students. Within weeks, it quickly becomes the go-to spot for campus life.

2019
Student-Approved Favorites

Expanding our menu to include more student classics—from crispy kuku and hot ugali to chapati beans—merging homestyle quality with fast service between classes.

2022
The Go-To Campus Hub

Becoming a staple of the JKUAT student community, serving hundreds of meals daily with the same warmth and affordability that started it all.
